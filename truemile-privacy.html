TrueMile: Mileage Log for Taxes — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 9, 2026

The short version

TrueMile works fully offline on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no internet permission at all.

What the app stores

Your mileage log — trips, vehicles, odometer readings, saved routes, rates, and any documents you file in the in-app Filing Cabinet — is stored locally on your device only, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it yourself (for example, saving a CSV report or printing a log to share with your tax preparer).

Location

If you record a trip with GPS, the app uses your device's location only while that trip is being recorded, and only on the device, to measure the distance driven. Location data is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared. You can also log every trip by odometer or by typing the miles — GPS is always optional, and you can turn the permission off in Android settings at any time.

Data collection and sharing

Data collected by us: None. Data shared with third parties: None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

Purchases

The app is free to download with a 7-day free trial. One optional purchase — the TrueMile Lifetime Unlock, $19.99 — is a single payment handled entirely by Google Play. It is not a subscription and never renews automatically. We do not see, receive, or store your payment information. Google's handling of your purchase data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
