DMV Practice Test: Car & CDL — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 5, 2026

The short version

DMV Permit & CDL Prep works fully offline on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.

What the app stores

Your practice progress — answers, results, readiness estimates, your missed-question review pile, and your chosen state and language — is stored locally on your device only, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device.

Data collection and sharing

Data collected by us: None. Data shared with third parties: None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

Purchases

The app is free to download, with a free starter set and free previews. Three optional purchases — 45-day passes for the Car + Signs, CDL and Motorcycle sections — are handled entirely by Google Play. Each pass is a single payment; it is not a subscription and never renews automatically. Pass time is stored on your device. We do not see, receive, or store your payment information. Google's handling of your purchase data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
