Home Care Sitter Sheet Memos — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 15, 2026

The short version

Private by design — everything stays on your phone. Home Care Sitter Sheet Memos works fully offline on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. We do not collect, upload, sell, or track any of your information.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — your household details, kids, pets, elders in your care, contacts, home information, and the sitter sheets you build — is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device on its own.

Data collection and sharing

Data collected by us: None. Data shared with third parties: None. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.

When information leaves your phone

Your information leaves the phone only when you tap Share, Print, or Save and choose where it goes — for example, sending a sitter sheet to your babysitter or printing it for the fridge. That choice is always yours, and the app sends nothing by itself.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. You can erase everything from inside the app's settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app — uninstalling the app deletes its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
