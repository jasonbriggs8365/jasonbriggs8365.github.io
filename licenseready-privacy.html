LicenseReady UP — Insurance License Exam Prep

The short version

This app works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server of ours. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information — there is nowhere for it to go.

What the app stores, and where

Your study progress — answers you have given, bookmarks, missed questions, readiness scores, and settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it yourself. If you use the export feature, the app saves a progress file to your own Downloads folder; what you do with that file is up to you.

Voice features

If you use voice practice, the app speaks questions aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech, and listens for your spoken answers using your device's built-in speech recognition service. Audio is handled by your device's own speech services under their own privacy policies. The app itself does not record audio, does not keep recordings, and does not send audio or transcripts to us or to any server of ours.

Microphone permission

The app asks for microphone access only so voice practice can hear your spoken answers. You can decline or revoke it at any time in your device settings; every other part of the app works without it.

Purchases

The optional paid pass is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Children

This app is study material for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam. It is not directed at children.
