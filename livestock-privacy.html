Livestock Ledger — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 3, 2026

The short version

Livestock Ledger stores everything on your phone and nowhere else. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.

What the app stores

All records you create — animals, health and breeding records, weigh-ins, sales, expenses, pastures and groups, photos you attach, documents you generate, and your keeper name and signature — are stored locally on your device only, in the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself (for example, a backup, CSV, or document you save or send).

Data collection and sharing

Data collected by us: None. Data shared with third parties: None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers.

Purchases

The app offers a free 15-day trial and an optional one-time lifetime unlock (product livestock_lifetime). All purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see, receive, or store your payment information. Google's handling of your purchase data is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): used only for reminders you set inside the app, scheduled on your device. Files / photos you pick: when you attach a photo or restore a backup, the app reads only the file you selected.

Deleting your data

Your records live only on your device. You can delete them by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
