Citizenship Test: Civics Pass — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 15, 2026

The short version

Private by design. Civics Pass works offline on your device. There is no account and no sign-in. We do not collect, upload, sell, or track any of your information. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.

What the app stores

Your study progress — your test setup, scores, missed questions, and bookmarks — is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device on its own, and we cannot see it.

Voice practice and the microphone

When you answer out loud, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition and text-to-speech services. Your voice is handled by your device's speech service under that service's own privacy policy. We do not record, receive, or store your voice. The microphone permission is used only at the moment you tap the microphone to answer. You can always type or tap your answers instead.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Links to official sites

When you tap a link to an official site (such as uscis.gov, senate.gov, or house.gov), your browser opens that site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Bug reports and email

If you tap the bug icon and email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem.

Children

Civics Pass is a study aid for adults preparing for the U.S. naturalization test. It is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app — uninstalling deletes its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
