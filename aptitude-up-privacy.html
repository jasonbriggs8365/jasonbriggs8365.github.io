Aptitude Up: Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026. Developer: Jason Briggs (Google Play developer name: Rezin92002).

Aptitude Up is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any government entity, including the U.S. Department of Defense or any military branch.

Summary

Aptitude Up does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking libraries.

Data stored on your device

Your practice answers, scores, streaks, study plan, planner dates and settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device only. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to copy, save or share a backup or a mistake report yourself.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers or advertising IDs.

Purchases

If paid passes are offered, purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment details.

Links to other websites

The app links to official websites (for example officialasvab.com and military recruiting sites). Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for the ASVAB, generally ages 17 and older. It collects no data from anyone.

Your control

You can delete all app data at any time by using Reset in the app's Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
