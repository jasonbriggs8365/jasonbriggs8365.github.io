NCLEX RN Pass: QBank + NGN — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 15, 2026

The short version

Private by design. NCLEX Pass works offline on your device. There is no account and no sign-in. We do not collect, upload, sell, or track any of your information. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.

What the app stores

Your study progress — your answers, scores, readiness index, missed questions, bookmarks, mock exam results, and study plan — is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device on its own, and we cannot see it. You can export a copy of your progress from the app's More screen whenever you like; where that file goes is up to you.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Links to official sites

When you tap a link to an official site (such as ncsbn.org or nclex.com), your browser opens that site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Bug reports and email

If you tap the bug icon or the “Report an error” link on a question and email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem.

Not medical advice

NCLEX Pass is an exam-preparation study aid. Its questions, explanations, and readiness index are for practice only and are not medical advice, clinical guidance, or a prediction of any exam result.

Children

NCLEX Pass is a study aid for nursing students and graduates preparing for the NCLEX-RN® examination. It is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app — uninstalling deletes its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
