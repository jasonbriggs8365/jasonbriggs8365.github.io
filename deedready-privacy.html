Real Estate Exam: DeedReady — Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 16, 2026

The short version

Private by design. DeedReady works offline on your device. There is no account and no sign-in. We do not collect, upload, sell, or track any of your information. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.

What the app stores

Your study progress — your exam state, answers, scores, wrong-answer review list, study plan, and settings — is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves your device on its own, and we cannot see it. If you use the export feature, the file is written to your device's Downloads folder only when you tap it, and stays under your control.

Listen mode, voice answering, and the microphone

Listen mode reads questions aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. When you answer out loud, the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition. Your voice is handled by your device's speech service under that service's own privacy policy. We do not record, receive, or store your voice. The microphone permission is used only at the moment you tap the microphone to answer, and the app asks for it in context, the first time you do. You can always tap your answers instead — voice is an add-on, never the only way to answer.

Purchases

The one-time lifetime unlock is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Links to official sites

When you tap a link to an official source (such as trec.texas.gov or myfloridalicense.com), your browser opens that site. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Bug reports and email

If you tap the bug icon and email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem.

Children

DeedReady is a study aid for adults preparing for a real estate salesperson licensing exam. It is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. You can erase your progress inside the app's settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app — uninstalling deletes its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
